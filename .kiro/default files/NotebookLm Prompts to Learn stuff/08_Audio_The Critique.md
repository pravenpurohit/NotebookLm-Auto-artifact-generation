Format: The Critique (two hosts)

Give a constructive critique of the material as if reviewing it for serious use.

Cover:
- What’s strong (clarity, evidence, completeness) — 5 points.
- What’s weak or unclear — 5 points.
- Hidden assumptions — 5–10.
- What’s missing that a reader would need to act on.
- Concrete improvements: “If you revise this, add/change X.”

Rules:
Be professional, not snarky. Stay grounded in sources.
