Create a detailed narrated video overview as a slide-based storyboard for [AUDIENCE].

Storyboard requirements:
- 8–12 slides total.
- Each slide must have:
  1) A short title (max 6 words)
  2) 2–4 on-screen bullets (no paragraphs)
  3) One visual idea (diagram/flowchart/icon/data callout) that matches the slide
- Slides must follow this arc:
  1) What this is + why it matters
  2) Key concepts (define 6–10 terms)
  3) The main framework/process (step-by-step)
  4) Evidence / key numbers
  5) Trade-offs / risks
  6) Real-world implications / decisions
  7) Summary + “what to do next”

Rules:
- Stay strictly grounded in sources.
- If sources conflict, include a slide titled “What’s disputed.”
- Prioritize clarity and retention over completeness.
