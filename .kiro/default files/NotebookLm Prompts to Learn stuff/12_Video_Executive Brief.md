Make a detailed executive briefing video.

Slides:
1) Executive summary (3 bullets)
2) What problem this solves (3 bullets)
3) Options compared (A/B/C) with trade-offs
4) Recommended option + rationale (from sources)
5) Risks & mitigations
6) Open questions / missing info
7) Next steps (30/60/90 day plan)

Rules:
- Make all claims source-grounded.
- Don’t add external context not in sources.
- Prefer crisp language and action verbs.
