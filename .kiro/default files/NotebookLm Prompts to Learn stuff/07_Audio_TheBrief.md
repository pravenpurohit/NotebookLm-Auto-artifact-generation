Format: The Brief (single speaker)

Deliver a crisp, high-information overview for [AUDIENCE] in under 2 minutes.

Include:
- 1-sentence definition of the topic.
- The 5 most important takeaways (one sentence each).
- 2 biggest risks or caveats.
- 3 “questions we should ask next.”

Rules:
No fluff. No hype. Only what’s supported by the sources.
